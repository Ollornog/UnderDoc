Customers, contacts and the like live in native lists instead of Excel.

**Done when this is true:**

- Lists have typed fields and links between lists, plus filter and sort.
- Rows can be edited in the web, with server-rendered forms and a history per row.
- Pages embed list views and single fields.
- Excel files can be imported once and exported on a schedule as a marked read-only copy.
- Personal data can be deleted including history and exports.
