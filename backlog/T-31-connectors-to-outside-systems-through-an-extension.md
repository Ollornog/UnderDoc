# T-31 — Connectors to outside systems through an extension point

Access to outside systems, shared or per person, stored encrypted. Reading and searching go through the common source search and read by URI (ADR-34); tools that act in an outside system come per connector and show only for people with access. A private connector plugs in at the extension point without changing UnderDoc. Timeouts per connector; every call logged.
