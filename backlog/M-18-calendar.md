Dates that already live in documents and lists appear on one calendar. They keep their single source.

**Done when this is true:**

- A calendar tab with month and agenda views.
- Entries come from date fields in native lists and from dates in front matter.
- External calendars can be read as ICS, under the rules for sources from M-4.
- A page can embed a calendar view.
- Each calendar is available as an ICS feed, with a personal token that can be revoked.
- Everything respects permissions.
- Deliberately no groupware: no invitations and no room booking.
