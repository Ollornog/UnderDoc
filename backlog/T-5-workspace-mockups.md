Pages that can be clicked through, with made-up content and C22 styling, in two or three variants per question. The current
navigation is the baseline. They are thrown away afterwards.

1. **Areas:** which tabs? Documents, Data, Database and Master data as four, or Documents and Data with the rest inside.
2. **Tree:** collections, how far folders open by themselves, how slim the list gets.
3. **Windows:** how to split the content area and how a document gets into a window (M-15).
4. **Table of contents per window:** side panel, hidden, or a marker with a drop-down.
5. **Facts panel:** right rail, tab or drawer, and how it looks in a narrow window.
6. **Page widths:** slim, wide and full.

**Done when:** the choice for each question is recorded in a decision, and M-14 and M-15 are updated to match.
