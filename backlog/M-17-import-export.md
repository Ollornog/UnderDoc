Documents come in and go out one at a time, from the web. Data exports and the API stay in M-7.

**Done when this is true:**

- Folders of Markdown, Word and HTML files can be imported, keeping the structure, images and internal links.
- Other formats, such as exports from OneNote or Confluence, only after their real export format has been checked.
- A document or folder can be exported as Markdown, PDF or Word.
- Save as creates a copy in another place, or a template.
- An export says which embedded data it froze, and passwords never leave (ADR-7).
