A site builder in the spirit of Google Sites: a site is a document type, and its children become its pages. T-8 settles
the details.

**Done when this is true:**

- A site has a navigation built from its children, a theme and a home page.
- Pages are built from sections with columns, which fits the rule against free placement.
- Publishing creates a fixed version with its own address, and changes stay private until the next publish.
- Published pages show frozen data, with all embedded objects listed for release, like public shares (M-5).
- Nothing on a published site loads from third parties.
- A site can be exported as static files.
